A participant without JavaScript therefore meets a plain, fully working control and the SERVER enforces the two steps (error_message on the task screen and on the quiz). Rendering the widget disabled server-side would strand them on a slider they could never satisfy. data-server-direction is what tells the script the two apart: EMPTY on a first view (the checked button is only the no-JS default, so clear it), and the participant's own direction on a re-render after a rejected submission (so restore it, with their bet). #}

{# 1. one intuition line #}

The more of your 100 points are on the island's actual type, the higher your chance of winning.

{# 2. the formula, then one worked example below it as plain text. PERCENTAGES ONLY (2026-08-11, Randolph feedback point 4): the panel used to introduce the share s = a / 100 and then mix fractions with percentages in the same three lines, so a reader had to convert units mid-explanation. Everything here is now in percent and the letter s is gone — do NOT reintroduce a share, a decimal or a 0-to-1 probability anywhere in this panel. THIS STILL MIRRORS main/scoring.py (binarised_win_prob = 1 - (1 - m)^2 with m = a / 100); keep the two in sync. The percent form below is that same rule multiplied by 100: 100 - (100 - a)^2 / 100. Checked against scoring.py at a = 0 / 50 / 70 / 100 (0 / 75 / 91 / 100 percent) in tests/test_ui_batch_http.py. The letter a keeps the accent colour (.info-a) on both its occurrences in the formula so its role reads at a glance. #}

With a of your 100 points on the actual type, your chance of winning is
100 − (100 − a)2 / 100 percent.

For example, if you put 70 of your 100 points on the actual type your chance of winning is 100 − 900 / 100 = 91 percent.

{# 3. answer-every-round reminder #}

You won't know which rounds pay, so answer every round as if it pays.

{# ---- STEP 2: how confident? ----------------------------------------- In the layout from the first paint, greyed and inert until step 1 is answered. ITS BOX IS RESERVED FROM THE FIRST PAINT, BY A GHOST.
